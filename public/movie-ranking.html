The Best Animes According to Atone

My top 3 animes of all-time.

Naruto

Let's start with this first show since I do not have a fond memory of movies I hold dear to heart. Naruto is a a show that hits my ultimate childhoor nostalgia and shows a kid who wanted to be the best and proved it. Naruto Shipuden be the main crowing and appreciation for the show.

One Piece

This is a show I got encouraged to watch by a coworker and told to watch from the very beginning to about 1200 episodes. Longest dedication I have ever made to a show and well worth. It is truly an outstanding show.

Solo Leveling

A hard decision between this show and One Punch Man but I have to hand it to the creator of this show it is a masterpiece and the leveling up aspect is amazing, just a like a video game. Just another representation of improving in the life and being the best.
